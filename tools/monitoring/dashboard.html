Definitions
  • Real users only: Play's test robots, sideloaded and nightly builds, and the owner's own account are left out of every number on this page.
  • New users are new accounts plus installs that never got an account. People logging back in to an older account aren't new.
  • Headline rates count users who reached the home screen, as the baseline does. A rate only includes users who have had long enough to reach it, e.g. 7 days for week one.
  • Setup on day one: kept the starter templates, or created a routine, exercise or plan, or started a plan. Tourist: no setup and no workout started on day one.
  • Queries, definitions and the refresh routine live in tools/monitoring in the app repo.
